Danny Ferris Yagan is the Robert D. Burch Professor of Economics at the University of California, Berkeley; Co-founder and Chief Economist of the Budget Lab at Yale; Director of the Berkeley Burch Center for Tax Policy and Public Finance; Research Associate of the National Bureau of Economic Research; and Co-editor and incoming Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Public Economics.
